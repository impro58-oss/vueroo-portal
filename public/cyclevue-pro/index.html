Scoring Methodology

Yield Curve (T10Y2Y)

  • > 1.0% = Score 25
  • 0% to 1.0% = Score 20
  • -0.5% to 0% = Score 15
  • -1.0% to -0.5% = Score 10
  • < -1.0% = Score 5

Fed Funds Rate

  • < 2% = Score 25
  • 2-4% = Score 20
  • 4-6% = Score 15
  • 6-8% = Score 10
  • > 8% = Score 5

M2 Growth (YoY)

  • > 8% = Score 25
  • 4-8% = Score 20
  • 0-4% = Score 15
  • -2-0% = Score 10
  • < -2% = Score 5

Credit Spread (BAA10Y)

  • < 1% = Score 25
  • 1-2% = Score 20
  • 2-3% = Score 15
  • 3-5% = Score 10
  • > 5% = Score 5

Interpretation: Scores are weighted by importance. Liquidity and Credit each contribute 30% of total score. Rates and Market contribute 20% each. A score below 60 indicates elevated risk. Historical crisis periods typically score 40-55.
