RPT-006 Weekly Synthesis Protocol

Department: RPT (Reporting) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-05-25 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE

Purpose

Compile weekly learnings, identify patterns, flag risks, and celebrate wins. Delivered Sunday 20:00 UTC.

Trigger

  • Weekly cron (Sunday 20:00 UTC)

    • Steps

    Step 1: Review the Week

  • Read all memory/ files created this week
  • Read all .learnings/ entries
  • Check cron job execution logs for failures
  • Review trading performance (RPT-002)
  • Review any incident responses (OPS-002)

    • Step 2: Identify Patterns

  • What themes emerged across multiple sessions?
  • What problems recurred?
  • What worked particularly well?
  • What surprised us?

    • Step 3: Compile the Synthesis

    markdown

    Weekly Synthesis — Week of [Date]
    🏆 Wins
    

  • [What went well this week]
    • 📈 Progress
    

  • [Projects that advanced]
    • 🔻 Setbacks
    

  • [What didn't work]
    • 🧠 Learnings
    

  • [New knowledge gained]
    • 

  • [Mistakes made and lessons from them]
    • ⚠️ Risks
    

  • [Emerging risks to watch]
    • 📋 Next Week Priorities
    
1. [Priority 1]
2. [Priority 2]
3. [Priority 3]

    Step 4: File and Deliver

  • Save to memory/synaptic/weekly-reflection-YYYY-MM-DD.md
  • Copy to Obsidian 50-Fireside-Chat-Repository/
  • Deliver to Telegram

    • Quality Gates

    All major events of the week covered
    At least 1 learning captured
    At least 1 risk flagged
    Next week priorities are specific and actionable

    References

  • memory/synaptic/ — Weekly reflection files
  • .learnings/ — Error and lesson logs
