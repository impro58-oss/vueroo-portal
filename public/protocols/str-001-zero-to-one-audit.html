← Back to Protocol Dashboard ﻿# STR-001 Zero to One Audit Protocol

Department: STR (Strategy) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-05-25 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE

Purpose

Classify every active project as 0→1 (creating something new) or 1→n (improving what exists). Allocate resources based on power-law returns. Kill projects that don't serve the mission.

Trigger

  • When starting a new quarter or strategic review
  • When feeling spread too thin across too many projects
  • When deciding where to invest time and money

    • Steps

    Step 1: List All Active Projects

    Pull from MEMORY.md active projects table.

    Step 2: Classify Each Project

    | Classification | Definition | Return Profile | Examples | |---------------|-----------|---------------|----------| | 0→1 | Creates something entirely new. Nobody does this. | Power-law (massive or zero) | Avaark, LUXBRIDGE | | 1→n Funding | Exists in market but generates revenue that funds 0→1 plays | Linear, reliable | NeuroVue, CryptoVue, AgentVue, TrojanVue | | 1→n Strategic | Exists in market but builds strategic capability | Linear + optionality | LotteryVue (research), 6B Doorway (MedTech positioning) | | Dead/Zombie | Not generating value, not building capability | Negative (drains time) | ComplyKeep (killed), stalled projects |

    Step 3: Apply the Power Law Test

    For each 0→1 project:
  • If this works, does it generate 10x+ the value of all 1→n projects combined?
  • If this fails, can we survive the loss?
  • Are we giving it enough resources to actually win?

    • For each 1→n project:

  • Does it generate revenue? How much?
  • Does it build capability we need for a 0→1 play?
  • If we killed it tomorrow, would anything important break?

    • Step 4: Resource Allocation

    Rule: 0→1 projects get disproportionate attention.

    Suggested allocation:

  • 60% of time → 0→1 plays (Avaark, LUXBRIDGE)
  • 30% of time → 1→n Funding (systems that generate revenue)
  • 10% of time → 1→n Strategic (capability builders)
  • 0% of time → Dead/Zombie (kill them)

    • Step 5: Kill List

    Projects that should be killed or mothballed:
  • Not generating revenue AND not building capability AND not a 0→1 play
  • Consuming >10% of time with <1% of returns
  • "We'll get to it someday" projects (they won't)

    Step 7: Present to Roo

  • The audit is a RECOMMENDATION, not a decision
  • Roo decides what to kill and what to keep
  • Document his decision and the reasoning
  • Update MEMORY.md and HEARTBEAT.md accordingly
