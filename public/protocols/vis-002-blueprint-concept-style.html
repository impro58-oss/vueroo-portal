Protocol VIS-002: Blueprint Concept Style — AIS Strategy Presentation Graphics

Created: 2026-08-05

Purpose: Define and preserve the hand-drawn blueprint concept illustration style developed for WallabyPhenox AIS strategy presentation deck, so future images maintain visual consistency.

Style Definition

Name: Blueprint Concept Sketch

Palette: Two-tone + white background

  • Primary: Dark navy blue — all structural linework, text, icons, annotations
  • Accent: Warm amber — used on exactly 2-3 key elements per image to draw the eye
  • Background: Pure white — generous white space, never coloured or textured

Line Quality:

  • Slightly wobbly, organic — like drawn quickly on tracing paper
  • Varying stroke thickness — not uniform digital lines
  • Some lines not quite meeting corners — intentional imperfection
  • A few faint "construction lines" left visible — suggests the design was thought through, not random
  • Cross-hatching for shadows where needed
  • No gradients, no fills (except amber accent dots), no glow, no photorealism, no 3D effects, no shadows

Typography:

  • Small handwritten-style labels — like an architect's margin notes
  • All caps for titles and corner annotations (e.g., "ROBOTIC CENTRE — CONCEPT v2")
  • Lowercase or mixed case for in-diagram labels
  • Minimal text — the visual does most of the talking

Composition:

  • Generous white space — images occupy 55-65% of frame
  • Single focal point per image
  • No clutter, no decorative elements
  • Each image has a small corner annotation (title + version/date)

Emotional Quality:

  • Feels like a concept sketch pinned to a wall during a strategy workshop
  • Accessible but technical — not corporate, not gimmicky
  • Human — the imperfections make it feel authored, not generated
  • Confident — the construction lines say "this was designed deliberately"

Key Design Decisions

  1. No company names on competitive images — use percentages only. Roo will add names himself if needed.
  1. Blank flag on figure — faceless figure with empty flag for Roo to write into.
  1. Fingerprint at 2036 — emotional anchor, not just a data point. Amber accent bookends (2026 dot ↔ 2036 fingerprint whorl).
  1. Amber used sparingly — exactly 2-3 elements per image. If everything is highlighted, nothing is.
  1. Construction lines visible — they say "designed deliberately," not "rough draft."
  1. No specific regions named on geographic expansion — abstract ripple pattern, no continents or country borders.

  • AIS Strategy Deck (WallabyPhenox) — primary use case
  • Investor presentations — same visual language
  • Internal strategy documents — consistent style
  • Future concept images — follow this protocol
