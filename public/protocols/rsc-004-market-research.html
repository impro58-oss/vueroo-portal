RSC-004 Market Research Protocol

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-05-25 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE

Purpose

Research market size (TAM/SAM/SOM), growth rates, and trends with proper sourcing. No fabricated financial data — every number must be tagged and traceable.

Trigger

  • When sizing a market for a business plan or investor deck
  • When evaluating a new opportunity (STR-003 Idea Development)
  • When updating competitive intelligence with market context

    • Prerequisites

  • Clear definition of the market segment being researched
  • Understanding of TAM/SAM/SOM framework
  • RSC-001 (Multi-Source Research) available for queries

    • Steps

    Step 1: Define the Market

    Write a market definition statement: 
    
Market: [specific segment, e.g., "neurovascular flow diverters in Europe"]
Geography: [global / regional / country-specific]
Time Period: [current year + 5-year forecast]
Customer: [who buys this — hospitals, clinics, patients, etc.]

    Step 2: Sizing — TAM / SAM / SOM

    TAM (Total Addressable Market): The total market demand if 100% of eligible customers bought. SAM (Serviceable Available Market): The segment TAM we can reach with our business model. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): The portion of SAM we can realistically capture.

    For each sizing level: 1. Search for existing market research reports (Grand View Research, Markets and Markets, Allied Market Research) 2. Cross-reference with public company revenue data (SEC filings for public companies in the space) 3. Calculate bottom-up: # of potential customers × average revenue per customer 4. Calculate top-down: total market × our segment percentage

    Tag every number:

  • From published report with methodology → [VERIFIED: Source, Year]
  • Calculated from company revenues → [ESTIMATE: derived from X, Y, Z sources]
  • No source available → [0.1 MARKER: no market data found]
  • NEVER fabricate a market size number

    • Step 3: Growth Rate

  • Find CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) from at least 2 sources
  • Note base year and forecast period
  • Tag: [VERIFIED: Source, base year-forecast year] or [ESTIMATE: derived from X]
  • Calculate yourself if needed: (end_value / start_value) ^ (1/years) - 1

    • Step 4: Trends and Drivers

    Identify: 1. Market drivers (what's pushing growth) — tag each 2. Market restraints (what's holding it back) — tag each 3. Technology trends (what's changing the landscape) — tag each 4. Regulatory environment (FDA, CE, country-specific) — tag each 5. Key events (M&A, new entrants, clinical trial results) — tag each

    Step 5: Synthesize

    Write the market brief:

    Quality Gates

    Market definition clearly stated
    TAM/SAM/SOM all calculated with sources
    Every financial number has a tag
    No fabricated market sizes (use 0.1 marker if no data)
    CAGR calculated or cited with base year
    At least 2 sources consulted

    Error Handling

    | Error | Response | |-------|----------| | Market reports contradict each other | Present both, note methodology differences, flag for Roo | | No published market data exists | Build bottom-up estimate, tag as [ESTIMATE], note gap | | Company revenue data unavailable (private co) | Use [ESTIMATE: industry average margin × known employee count], note uncertainty |
