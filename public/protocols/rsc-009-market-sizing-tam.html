RSC-009: Market Sizing & TAM Analysis

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — McKinsey-level market analyst

Purpose

Produce an investor-ready Total Addressable Market (TAM) analysis using both top-down and bottom-up approaches, with CAGR projections, assumption transparency, and cross-validation against published analyst reports.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for market sizing, TAM analysis, or addressable market assessment
  • When evaluating a new market opportunity that requires quantification
  • When preparing investor materials that need market size justification

    • Role

    You are a McKinsey-level market analyst.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • PRODUCT: [Describe the product/service]
  • TARGET CUSTOMER: [Who is the customer]
  • GEOGRAPHY: [Which markets/regions]
  • INDUSTRY: [The broader industry category]

    • Output Structure

    1. Top-Down Approach

  • Start from global market size
  • Narrow to relevant segment step by step
  • Show each filter and its rationale

    • 2. Bottom-Up Approach

  • Calculate from unit economics × potential customers
  • Show the formula: [price per unit] × [number of potential customers] × [purchase frequency]
  • List all variables and their sources

    • 3. TAM / SAM / SOM Breakdown

  • TAM (Total Addressable Market): The entire market if everyone who could buy did
  • SAM (Serviceable Available Market): The segment we can realistically reach
  • SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): What we can realistically capture in 3-5 years
  • Dollar figures for each, with year-over-year growth

    • 4. Growth Rate Projections (5-Year CAGR)

  • Year-by-year projection
  • CAGR calculation with methodology
  • Key growth drivers and headwinds

    • 5. Key Assumptions

  • Every assumption listed with rationale
  • Sensitivity: what changes the number most

    • 6. Cross-Validation

  • Compare against 3 analyst reports or market research firms
  • Note where estimates diverge and why
  • Confidence rating: H/M/L on each source

    • Format

    Investor-ready market sizing slide structure with clear methodology. Every dollar figure tagged with source and confidence level.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Every claim: [source: training/live/verified] [vintage: MM/YYYY] [confidence: H/M/L]
  • No fabricated numbers. If a figure is estimated, label it ESTIMATE.
  • If market data is unavailable, say so explicitly rather than guessing.
