Shark Tank Pitch Deck Protocol

Purpose: Generate consistent 4-slide Shark Tank pitch decks for medical device concept proposals. Built on the Stanford Biodesign framework — unmet need statement, concept rationale, and opportunity assessment.

Created: 2026-07-10

Version: 1.0

Structure: 4 Slides

Slide 1: The Need (Biodesign Unmet Need Statement)

Purpose: State the clinical problem clearly, without embedding the solution. Frame the pain.

Content:

  • Unmet need statement (1-2 sentences, biodesign format)
  • Disease state context (1-2 key statistics)
  • Current treatment limitations (what's wrong today)
  • No solution embedded — just the problem

Biodesign need statement format:

"[Disease/condition] patients undergoing [procedure] experience [problem] because [root cause], leading to [consequence]."

Example (braided stent):

"Physicians treating bifurcation and dissecting intracranial aneurysms lack a purpose-built braided stent with adequate wall coverage, X-ray visibility, and coating — forcing them to use legacy devices (LEO, 16 wires) or off-label peripheral stents that are too stiff and lack endothelium integration."

Slide 2: The Concept (What We're Proposing)

Purpose: Present the device concept as the answer to the need. What is it, why this design, what makes it different.

Content:

  • Device name/concept title
  • What it is (1-2 sentences, simple terms)
  • Key design features (3-4 bullets, no jargon)
  • Why this design over alternatives (braided vs laser-cut, 36 wires, HPC, DFT)
  • The differentiator (what no competitor has)
  • Concept artwork (hybrid blueprint-Foose style image if available)

Slide 3: Why We Should Do This (The Case for Action)

Purpose: The strategic argument. Why this product, why now, why us.

Content:

  • Market pull (physician demand quotes, global confirmation)
  • Three indications, one device (efficiency story)
  • Competitive gap (what's missing in the market)
  • Company capability (we can build this — existing tech, experience)
  • Risk assessment (low — similar to Pegasus path)
  • Strategic urgency (dissection growing, no good stent exists)

Slide 4: Opportunity Assessment (The Numbers)

Purpose: Data-driven summary from VOT calls, research, and market analysis. This slide is populated from opportunity assessment work — not the concept story.

Content:

  • Market sizing (units/year by region)
  • Pricing/ASP by region
  • Indication breakdown (% Y-stent, dissection, framing)
  • Competitive landscape table
  • Regulatory pathway summary
  • Timeline to market
  • Key assumptions and confidence levels

Note: Slide 4 is a placeholder during early concept stage. It gets filled as opportunity assessment data is gathered from VOT calls, market research, and team inputs.

Pitch Deck Generation Rules

  1. Slides 1-3 are the story — written for an external audience (Shark Tank panel of experts)
  1. Slide 4 is the evidence — data-dense, for the internal team and decision-makers
  1. Language: British English for all prose
  1. Tone: Confident, clinical, evidence-based. No hype. Let the unmet need speak for itself.
  1. Quotes: Direct physician/team quotes where available (from VOT/VOC calls)
  1. Artwork: Use hybrid blueprint-Foose concept images where available
  1. No fluff: Every bullet earns its place. If it doesn't serve the argument, cut it.

Data Sources for Each Slide

Slide Primary Source Secondary Source
1 — The Need VOT call notes + biodesign need statement format Stroke research brain notes, clinical literature
2 — The Concept VOT call (design specs, sizing, features) Concept artwork (hybrid blueprint-Foose)
3 — Why Do This VOT call (market pull, competitive gap, physician quotes) Competitor analysis, market research
4 — Assessment Opportunity assessment data (separate workstream) VOT pricing/ASP data, regulatory research

Template Variables

When generating a pitch deck, fill in:

[CONCEPT_NAME] — e.g., "36-Wire HPC-Coated Braided Stent"
[INDICATIONS] — e.g., "Y-stent, Dissection, Framing for FD"
[UNMET_NEED_STATEMENT] — biodesign format
[DISEASE_STATS] — key numbers from research
[CURRENT_LIMITATIONS] — what's wrong today
[DEVICE_DESCRIPTION] — simple terms
[KEY_FEATURES] — 3-4 bullets
[DIFFERENTIATOR] — what no competitor has
[PHYSICIAN_QUOTES] — direct quotes from VOT/VOC
[MARKET_SIZE] — units/year by region
[ASP_BY_REGION] — pricing
[INDICATION_SPLIT] — % breakdown
[COMPETITOR_TABLE] — product comparison
[REGULATORY_PATH] — 510(k)/MDR/De Novo
[TIMELINE] — to first-in-human
[RISK_LEVEL] — low/medium/high with reason

PowerPoint Formatting

  • Aspect ratio: 16:9
  • Font: Sora for headers, Maison Neue for body (fallback: Calibri)
  • Header colour: RGB(0, 51, 102) — dark navy
  • Body colour: RGB(51, 51, 51) — dark grey
  • Accent colour: RGB(0, 120, 160) — medical blue
  • Background: White
  • No animation — static slides for review
  • Slide 1: Title + need statement + 2-3 key stats
  • Slide 2: Title + concept description + features + artwork
  • Slide 3: Title + 4-5 strategic arguments with quotes
  • Slide 4: Title + data tables + key numbers
