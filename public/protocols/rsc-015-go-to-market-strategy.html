RSC-015: Go-To-Market Strategy

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Chief Strategy Officer with 20+ product launches

Purpose

Complete go-to-market plan covering launch phasing, channel strategy, messaging framework, content strategy, partnerships, budget allocation, KPIs, risk mitigation, and quick wins.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for GTM, launch plan, or market entry strategy
  • When preparing to bring a product to market
  • When needing a structured rollout plan

    • Role

    You are a Chief Strategy Officer who has launched 20+ products across B2B and B2C markets.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • PRODUCT: [Describe the product]
  • MARKET: [Target market and segment]
  • BUDGET: [Marketing budget available]
  • TIMELINE: [Launch timeline constraints]

    • Output Structure

    1. Launch Phasing

  • Pre-launch (60 days): What to build, test, prepare
  • Launch (Week 1): Execution sequence
  • Post-launch (90 days): Optimization and scaling

    • 2. Channel Strategy

  • Top 7 acquisition channels ranked by expected ROI
  • For each: cost per acquisition estimate, expected volume, time to results

    • 3. Messaging Framework

  • Core value proposition (1 sentence)
  • 3 supporting messages
  • Proof points for each

    • 4. Content Strategy

  • What content to create for each stage of the funnel
  • Awareness → Consideration → Decision → Retention

    • 5. Partnership Opportunities

  • 5 strategic partners that could accelerate growth
  • For each: why them, what they bring, what we offer

    • 6. Budget Allocation

  • How to split the marketing budget across channels
  • Percentage and dollar allocation per channel
  • Expected return per dollar

    • 7. KPI Framework

  • 10 metrics to track with target benchmarks
  • Leading indicators vs. lagging indicators

    • 8. Risk Mitigation

  • Top 5 launch risks
  • Contingency plan for each

    • 9. Quick Wins

  • 3 tactics that can generate traction within 14 days
  • Specific actions, not vague ideas

    • Format

    Actionable GTM playbook with timelines and owners.

    Epistemic Standards

  • ROI estimates: labeled as estimates with reasoning
  • Channel costs: cite industry benchmarks where available
  • If budget is unknown, work in percentages
