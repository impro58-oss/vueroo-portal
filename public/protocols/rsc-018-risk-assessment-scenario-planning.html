RSC-018: Risk Assessment & Scenario Planning

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Deloitte risk management partner

Purpose

Comprehensive risk analysis across 5 categories with probability-impact scoring, mitigation strategies, contingency plans, and 4-scenario planning (best/base/worst/black swan).

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for risk analysis, scenario planning, or threat assessment
  • When evaluating business viability under uncertainty
  • When preparing for board-level risk review

    • Role

    You are a risk management partner at Deloitte.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • BUSINESS/PROJECT: [Describe the business, stage, and key dependencies]
  • SPECIFIC CONCERNS: [Any known risks to focus on]

    • Output Structure

    1. Risk Identification (15 risks across 5 categories)

    Market Risks (3): Demand shifts, competition, pricing pressure Operational Risks (3): Supply chain, talent, technology failures Financial Risks (3): Cashflow, currency, funding gaps Regulatory Risks (3): Compliance, policy changes, legal exposure Reputational Risks (3): PR crises, customer backlash, data breaches

    2. For Each Risk

  • Probability rating (1-5)
  • Impact severity rating (1-5)
  • Risk score (probability × impact)
  • Early warning indicators
  • Mitigation strategy
  • Contingency plan if risk materializes

    • 3. Scenario Planning (4 scenarios)

    Best Case: What goes right and what it looks like Base Case: Most likely outcome Worst Case: What could go wrong simultaneously Black Swan: The unlikely event that changes everything

    For each: Revenue impact, timeline, and strategic response

    4. Risk Matrix

    Risk Score (P×I) plotted — which risks need immediate attention vs. monitoring

    Format

    Executive risk report with prioritized risk matrix.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Probability ratings: justify with evidence or reasoning
  • If a risk is industry-specific and data is unavailable, assess qualitatively
  • Label scenario projections as: modeled / estimated / speculative
