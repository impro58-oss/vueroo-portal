WP Naming Convention

Lowercase "p" prefix + UPPERCASE product name. No spaces.

Key Corrections

  • pRELACS does not exist. It is pRELAX (vasospasm device).
  • Prelux (discontinued) should be pRELUX if it followed WP convention.
  • pitta/pITTA = balloon catheter, OEM gap product.
