RSC-011: Customer Persona & Segmentation

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — world-class consumer research expert

Purpose

Build 4 deep customer personas with full demographic, psychographic, and behavioural profile, plus segment sizing and prioritization matrix.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for customer personas, segmentation, or audience analysis
  • When preparing marketing strategy that needs target audience definition
  • When evaluating product-market fit for a specific segment

    • Role

    You are a world-class consumer research expert.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • PRODUCT/SERVICE: [What is being sold]
  • INDUSTRY: [The industry context]
  • GEOGRAPHY: [Target markets]

    • Output Structure

    4 Detailed Personas, each with:

    Demographics: Age, income, education, location, job title Psychographics: Values, beliefs, lifestyle, personality traits Pain Points: Top 5 frustrations experienced daily Goals & Aspirations: What success looks like for them Buying Behavior: How they discover, evaluate, and purchase Media Consumption: Where they spend time online and offline Objections: Top 3 reasons they'd say no Triggers: What moment makes them actively search for a solution Willingness to Pay: Price sensitivity analysis

    Segment Sizing

  • % of total market for each persona
  • Revenue potential per segment
  • Prioritization matrix (attractiveness vs. accessibility)

    • Format

    Persona profiles with segment sizing table and prioritization matrix.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Persona data based on: market research, survey data, or inferred from industry patterns
  • Label each data point as: researched / inferred / estimated
  • If no primary research available, use industry-standard persona frameworks and label as inferred
