RSC-010: Competitive Landscape Deep Dive

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Bain & Company senior strategy consultant

Purpose

Produce a complete competitive landscape analysis with direct and indirect competitors, positioning maps, moat assessment, white space analysis, and threat ratings.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for competitive analysis, landscape mapping, or competitor intelligence
  • When entering a new market and needing to understand the playing field
  • When evaluating competitive positioning or differentiation

    • Role

    You are a senior strategy consultant at Bain & Company.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • INDUSTRY: [The industry/market being analyzed]
  • YOUR COMPANY: [Describe your business and positioning]
  • GEOGRAPHY: [Relevant markets]

    • Output Structure

    1. Direct Competitors (Top 10)

    For each: company name, market share, revenue, funding, pricing model, key features, target audience, strengths, weaknesses, recent strategic moves
  • Ranked by market share

    • 2. Indirect Competitors (5)

    Adjacent companies that could enter this market
  • For each: why they're a threat, what would entry look like, probability of entry

    • 3. Market Positioning Map

  • Price vs. Value matrix (text-described, can be rendered as table)
  • X-axis: Price (low to high)
  • Y-axis: Value/Features (basic to premium)
  • Each competitor plotted

    • 4. Competitive Moats

  • What makes each player defensible
  • Moat type: network effects, brand, switching costs, scale, IP, regulatory

    • 5. White Space Analysis

  • Gaps no competitor is filling
  • Segment underserved by current players
  • Feature gaps across the landscape

    • 6. Threat Assessment

    Each competitor rated: Low / Medium / High
  • With reasoning for the rating
  • Direction of threat (expanding, stable, declining)

    • Format

    Structured competitive intelligence report with comparison tables.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Market share figures: [source] [vintage] [confidence]
  • Revenue figures: note if estimated vs. reported
  • If competitor data is unavailable, say so — do not fabricate
