RPT-003 Business Plan Protocol

Department: RPT (Reporting) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-05-25 Owner: Lumina Status: DRAFT

Purpose

Write a business plan that follows the Zero to One framework. Every business plan must answer: Is this 0→1 or 1→n? What's the secret? Where's the monopoly?

Trigger

  • When starting a new venture (Repurpose.ai, Avaark, etc.)
  • When preparing for investors or partners
  • When evaluating whether to continue a project (STR-001 audit)

    • Prerequisites

  • STR-003 Idea Development completed (idea validated)
  • Market research completed (RSC-004) or at least scoped
  • Clear understanding of the product and target customer

    • Steps

    Step 1: Zero to One Classification

    Before writing ANYTHING, classify:
  • 0→1: Creating something entirely new. Nobody does this. Power-law returns.
  • 1→n: Improving something that exists. Others compete. Linear returns.

    • This classification determines the ENTIRE plan's strategy.

    Step 2: The Seven Questions (from Thiel)

    Answer ALL seven. Each gets a strength rating 1-5:

    1. Engineering: Can you create breakthrough technology instead of incremental improvements? 2. Timing: Is now the right time? What changed recently? 3. Monopoly: Are you starting with a big share of a small market? 4. People: Do you have the right team? 5. Distribution: Do you have a way to reach customers? 6. Durability: Will this be defensible in 10-20 years? 7. Secret: Have you identified a unique opportunity that others don't see?

    Total score: /35. Below 20 = rethink. 20-25 = proceed with caution. 25+ = strong foundation.

    Step 3: Alchemical Stage

    Where is this venture in the transformation cycle?

    | Stage | Meaning | What to Do | |-------|---------|-----------| | Nigredo | Breaking down the old | Research, validate, let old assumptions die | | Albedo | Purifying, separating | Test ruthlessly, kill weak parts, refine | | Citrinitas | Illumination, the insight | Document the secret, don't build yet | | Rubedo | Integration, bringing to form | Build, ship, operate |

    Step 5: Validate Assumptions

    For each key assumption in the plan: 1. Is it based on verified data or guesswork? 2. What's the cheapest way to test it? 3. What would disprove it?

    Step 6: Review

  • Self-review using Recursive Excellence (score on accuracy, logic, clarity, depth, relevance, actionability)
  • If score < 7/10, revise before presenting to Roo
  • Multi-model review if HIGH-stakes (investor-facing)

    • Quality Gates

    0→1 or 1→n clearly classified
    Seven Questions answered with strength ratings
    Alchemical stage identified
    Market sizes tagged [VERIFIED/ESTIMATE]
    Financial projections tagged (no untagged projections)
    Key assumptions identified and testable
    Self-review score ≥ 7/10
