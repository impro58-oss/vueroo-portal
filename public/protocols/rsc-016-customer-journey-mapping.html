RSC-016: Customer Journey Mapping

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — customer experience strategist at top consulting firm

Purpose

Complete customer journey map covering every stage from awareness to churn, with actions, emotions, touchpoints, pain points, opportunities, and metrics per stage.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for customer journey, experience mapping, or lifecycle analysis
  • When optimizing conversion funnel or reducing churn
  • When designing onboarding or retention strategy

    • Role

    You are a customer experience strategist at a top consulting firm.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • PRODUCT/SERVICE: [What is being mapped]
  • CUSTOMER TYPE: [Which customer segment]
  • CURRENT CONVERSION RATE: [If known]

    • Output Structure

    Journey Stages (7)

    For each stage provide: Customer actions, thoughts, emotions, touchpoints, pain points, opportunities, metrics, and recommended tactics.

    1. Awareness: How do they first discover us? What triggers the search? 2. Consideration: What do they compare? What information do they need? 3. Decision: What makes them convert? What almost stops them? 4. Onboarding: First 7 days experience that builds or kills retention 5. Engagement: What keeps them coming back? Key activation moments? 6. Loyalty: What turns users into advocates? Referral triggers? 7. Churn: Why do they leave? Early warning signals?

    Emotional Curve

  • Text-described emotional trajectory across all stages
  • Highs and lows identified
  • Friction points where emotion drops

    • Summary Table

    | Stage | Key Metric | Current Performance | Target | Priority |

    Format

    Detailed journey map with emotional curve described in text.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Journey stages based on: customer research, analytics data, or industry frameworks
  • Label data source for each stage insight
  • If no primary research, use industry-standard journey frameworks and label as inferred
