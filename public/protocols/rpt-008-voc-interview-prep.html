RPT-008: VOC Interview Preparation Protocol

Physician Interview Talk Track & Guide

Purpose

Produce a professional, well-structured VOC (Voice of Customer) interview guide for physician interviews. These guides ensure consistent, high-quality intelligence gathering that can be compared across KOLs and synthesized into actionable product insights.

When to Use

  • Preparing for a physician VOC interview (any specialty)
  • Creating a Talk Track for stakeholder calls
  • Developing structured questions for customer discovery
  • Building interview guides for competitive intelligence gathering

Pre-Work (MANDATORY)

Step 1: Physician Research (15 min minimum)

Before writing a single question, gather:

Step 2: Interview Context

Document in the guide:

  • Call duration (e.g., 45 minutes)
  • Interview format (phone, video, in-person)
  • Strategic goal (what decision this informs)
  • Your relationship (cold outreach, warm intro, existing partner)
  • Prior interactions (have you met? When?)

Question Design Rules

DO

  • Lead with open-ended questions
  • Use their professional language (from their publications/bios)
  • Ask for specific examples: "Can you walk me through a recent case..."
  • Include follow-up prompts: "Tell me more about that..."
  • Balance structure with flexibility (70% structured, 30% exploratory)

DO NOT

  • Ask yes/no questions as primary questions
  • Include more than 3 questions per sub-topic (overwhelming)
  • Use jargon they don't use
  • Ask questions you can answer from public sources
  • Lead the witness ("Don't you think X is better?")

Delivery Standards

Output Files (MANDATORY)

  1. Word document — primary deliverable for interview use
  2. Markdown — backup, Obsidian vault copy
  3. Both files copied to: Workspace memory/ directory and Obsidian vault 20-Areas/MedTech-NeuroVue/VOC-Intelligence/

Pre-Interview Checklist

  • Physician research completed
  • Prior VOC data reviewed (if any)
  • Questions tailored to their specific profile
  • Time allocations add up correctly
  • Word document opens and displays correctly
  • Files copied to vault

Post-Interview (MANDATORY FOLLOW-UP)

Within 24 hours:

  • Transcribe notes / recording
  • Extract verbatim quotes
  • Tag insights: Device | Technique | Unmet Need | Competitive Intel
  • Update physician profile in KOL tracker
  • File in vault: VOC-[Physician]-[Date]-Notes.md
  • Cross-reference with prior VOCs (synthesis opportunity)

Quality Gates (Three Gates)

Gate 1: Accuracy

  • Physician research complete?
  • All facts about them verified?
  • Prior VOC data reviewed?

Gate 2: Completeness

  • All strategic topics covered?
  • Time allocations correct (sum to total)?
  • Opening and closing sections present?
  • Questions are open-ended?

Gate 3: Delivery

  • Word document opens correctly?
  • Markdown version created?
  • Files in correct vault location?
  • Ready for interview use?
