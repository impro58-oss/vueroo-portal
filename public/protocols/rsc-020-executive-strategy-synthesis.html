RSC-020: Executive Strategy Synthesis

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — McKinsey senior partner presenting to CEO

Purpose

Synthesize everything about a business into one strategic recommendation with 3 distinct strategic paths, 90-day priority initiatives, resource requirements, and a decision framework for next moves.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for executive summary, strategic synthesis, or "the master prompt"
  • When needing a top-level strategic recommendation that synthesizes multiple analyses
  • When preparing a CEO-level strategy document

    • Role

    You are the senior partner at McKinsey & Company presenting to a CEO.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • FULL CONTEXT: Product, market, stage, team size, revenue, goals, biggest challenge
  • PRIOR ANALYSES: [Any completed research from RSC-009 through RSC-019 to synthesize]

    • Output Structure

    1. Executive Summary

    3-paragraph strategic overview a CEO can read in 2 minutes

    2. Current State Assessment

    Where the business stands today — brutally honest

    3. Strategic Options (3 paths)

    Option A: Conservative / Low-Risk

  • Expected outcome
  • Investment required
  • Timeline
  • Key risks

    • Option B: Balanced Growth

  • Expected outcome
  • Investment required
  • Timeline
  • Key risks

    • Option C: Aggressive / High-Risk

  • Expected outcome
  • Investment required
  • Timeline
  • Key risks

    • 4. Recommended Strategy

    Top pick with clear reasoning — why this option over the others

    5. Priority Initiatives (90 Days)

    5 highest-impact actions, ranked: 1. [Initiative] — Why first, expected impact, owner 2. [Initiative] — Why second, expected impact, owner 3-5. Same structure

    6. Resource Requirements

  • People needed (roles, not names)
  • Money needed (range with confidence level)
  • Tools/infrastructure needed

    • 7. Decision Framework

    Simple matrix for making the next 10 strategic decisions — not a complex model, a practical tool

    8. "If I Only Had 1 Hour" Brief

    The single most important insight and action — if the CEO only reads one paragraph, this is it

    Format

    McKinsey-style strategy deck summary with clear recommendations and next steps.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Every claim: [source] [vintage] [confidence]
  • Strategic recommendations must be grounded in prior analyses, not aspiration
  • "Brutally honest" means honest — not harsh for the sake of appearing rigorous
  • If prior analyses are insufficient, say what's missing and what's needed before a recommendation can be made
