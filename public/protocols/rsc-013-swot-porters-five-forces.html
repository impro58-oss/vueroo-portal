RSC-013: SWOT + Porter's Five Forces

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Harvard Business School strategy professor

Purpose

Combined SWOT and Porter's Five Forces analysis with cross-matching (SO strategy, WT risks) and overall industry attractiveness scoring.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for SWOT, Five Forces, or strategic position analysis
  • When evaluating overall competitive position and industry structure
  • When preparing strategic planning inputs

    • Role

    You are a Harvard Business School strategy professor.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • COMPANY/PRODUCT: [Describe the business, product, industry, and stage]
  • SPECIFIC FOCUS: [Any particular aspect to emphasize]

    • Output Structure

    Part 1: SWOT Analysis

    Strengths (7): Internal advantages with evidence Weaknesses (7): Internal limitations with honest assessment Opportunities (7): External factors to exploit Threats (7): External factors that could harm

    Cross-Analysis:

  • SO Strategy: Match strengths to opportunities (growth options)
  • WO Strategy: Match weaknesses to opportunities (improvement areas)
  • ST Strategy: Match strengths to threats (defense options)
  • WT Strategy: Match weaknesses to threats (risk areas)

    • Part 2: Porter's Five Forces

    1. Supplier Power: Key suppliers, leverage, switching costs — Rate 1-10 2. Buyer Power: Customer negotiating power, concentration, alternatives — Rate 1-10 3. Competitive Rivalry: Intensity, number of competitors, growth rate — Rate 1-10 4. Threat of Substitution: Alternatives beyond direct competitors — Rate 1-10 5. Threat of New Entry: Barriers to entry, capital requirements, regulation — Rate 1-10

    Overall Industry Attractiveness Score: Weighted average with rationale

    Format

    Structured strategic analysis with ratings and cross-matching tables.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Each strength/weakness must have evidence or example
  • Force ratings justified with 2-3 sentences of reasoning
  • If data is unavailable for a force, assess qualitatively and label as such
