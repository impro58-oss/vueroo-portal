RSC-012: Industry Trend Analysis

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Goldman Sachs Research senior analyst

Purpose

Comprehensive trend report covering macro forces, micro trends, technology disruptions, regulatory shifts, consumer behavior changes, and investment signals — with impact ratings and timeline mapping.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for industry trends, market analysis, or sector outlook
  • When evaluating strategic direction against market trajectory
  • When preparing for strategic planning or investment decisions

    • Role

    You are a senior analyst at Goldman Sachs Research.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • INDUSTRY: [The sector being analyzed]
  • YOUR BUSINESS: [Describe your business and market position]
  • GEOGRAPHY: [Global or specific regions]

    • Output Structure

    1. Macro Trends (5)

    Global forces shaping this industry:
  • Economic, Regulatory, Technological, Social, Environmental
  • Each with impact rating (1-10) and timeline

    • 2. Micro Trends (7)

    Emerging patterns within the industry from the last 12 months:
  • Each with description, evidence, and implication

    • 3. Technology Disruptions

  • What new tech is changing the game
  • When it will hit mainstream (timeline)
  • Impact on existing business models

    • 4. Regulatory Shifts

  • Upcoming legislation or policy changes
  • Expected impact on market participants
  • Timeline to compliance

    • 5. Consumer Behavior Changes

  • How buyer preferences are evolving
  • Evidence from data or research
  • Direction of change

    • 6. Investment Signals

  • Where smart money is flowing (VC deals, M&A, IPOs)
  • What investment patterns tell us about future direction
  • Hot sectors vs. cooling sectors

    • 7. Timeline Map

    Each trend mapped to:
  • Short-term (0-1 yr)
  • Mid-term (1-3 yr)
  • Long-term (3-5 yr)

    • 8. "So What" Analysis

    What each trend means for a company like Roo's — practical implications, not abstract observations.

    Format

    Trend intelligence brief with impact ratings (1-10) for each trend.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Trend data: [source] [vintage] [confidence]
  • Investment data: cite specific deals or reports where possible
  • Distinguish between: established trends (data-backed) vs. emerging signals (anecdotal)
