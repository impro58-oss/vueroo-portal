RPT-004 Presentation (PPTX) Protocol

Department: RPT (Reporting) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-05-25 Owner: Lumina Status: DRAFT

Purpose

Create a slide deck that communicates clearly, looks professional, and follows a proven structure.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for a presentation or pitch deck
  • When preparing for GEST, investor meetings, or partner calls

    • Prerequisites

  • Clear purpose: what decision should the audience make?
  • Key messages defined
  • Data/visuals ready

    • Steps

    Step 1: Define the Deck

    
Title: [Deck name]
Audience: [Investors / Partners / Conference / Internal]
Purpose: [What decision or action do you want?]
Length: [Number of slides — typically 10-15 for investor, 5-7 for update]
Tone: [Professional / Visionary / Technical]

    Step 2: Structure the Deck

    Use the proven 7-slide structure (expandable):

    | Slide | Content | Time | |-------|---------|------| | 1. Title | Company name, tagline, date | 10 sec | | 2. Problem | Who hurts and how much | 60 sec | | 3. Solution | What you build and why it's different | 90 sec | | 4. Market | TAM/SAM/SOM, growth rate | 60 sec | | 5. Traction | What you've achieved, metrics | 60 sec | | 6. Team | Who's building this | 30 sec | | 7. Ask | What you need and what you'll do with it | 60 sec |

    Step 3: Design Standards

  • Dark background (#0a0e17 or #111827) with light text (#e2e8f0)
  • Accent color for emphasis (indigo #6366f1 or teal #14b8a6)
  • Maximum 6 bullet points per slide
  • Maximum 1 chart or visual per slide
  • Font: system-ui or Inter, minimum 18pt
  • No walls of text — if you can't say it in 6 lines, split it

    • Step 4: Build with PPTX Skill

    Use the create-pptx or pptx-generator skill to generate the deck programmatically. This ensures consistency and repeatability.

    Step 5: Review

    Every slide has a single clear message
    Data is labeled and sourced
    No more than 6 bullets per slide
    Charts are readable from 10 feet
    The ask is explicit and specific

    Quality Gates

    Structure follows 7-slide format (or close)
    Every slide has one clear message
    Data is sourced and tagged
    Visual design is consistent
    Ask slide is explicit
