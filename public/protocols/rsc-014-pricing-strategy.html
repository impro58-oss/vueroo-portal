RSC-014: Pricing Strategy Analysis

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — Fortune 500 pricing strategy consultant

Purpose

Comprehensive pricing analysis including competitive audit, value-based pricing, cost-plus floor, elasticity, psychological strategies, tiering, discount strategy, and revenue projection scenarios.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for pricing analysis, strategy, or optimization
  • When launching a new product and determining price point
  • When repositioning or restructuring existing pricing

    • Role

    You are a pricing strategy consultant who has worked with Fortune 500 companies.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • PRODUCT: [Describe the product/service]
  • CURRENT PRICE: [If any — current pricing structure]
  • TARGET CUSTOMER: [Who pays]
  • COST STRUCTURE: [Fixed and variable costs, gross margin]

    • Output Structure

    1. Competitive Pricing Audit

  • Map all competitor prices, tiers, and packaging
  • Table: Competitor | Price | Tier | Features | Positioning

    • 2. Value-Based Pricing Model

  • Calculate price based on customer value delivered
  • Value metrics: time saved, revenue generated, cost avoided
  • Price = [value delivered] × [value capture ratio]

    • 3. Cost-Plus Analysis

  • Determine floor price from cost structure
  • Margin at various price points

    • 4. Price Elasticity Estimate

  • How sensitive is demand to price changes
  • Elasticity coefficient estimate with reasoning

    • 5. Psychological Pricing Strategies

  • Anchoring, charm pricing, decoy strategies
  • Application to this specific product

    • 6. Tiering Recommendation

  • Design 3 pricing tiers with feature allocation
  • Tier names, prices, included features, target segment

    • 7. Discount Strategy

  • When to discount, how much, and for whom
  • Discount types: volume, annual, promotional, loyalty

    • 8. Revenue Projection

  • 3 scenarios: Aggressive, Moderate, Conservative
  • Revenue at each price point × expected volume

    • 9. Monetization Opportunities

  • Upsells, cross-sells, usage-based pricing
  • Add-on revenue streams

    • Format

    Pricing strategy deck with specific dollar recommendations.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Competitor prices: cite source and date
  • Elasticity estimates: label as estimated, show reasoning
  • Revenue projections: show assumptions explicitly
