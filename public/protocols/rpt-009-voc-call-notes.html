RPT-009: VOC Call Notes Protocol

Post-Interview Summary Document

Purpose

Transform raw VOC interview transcripts into structured, professional summary documents that capture key insights, quotes, and actionable intelligence for upstream marketing and R&D teams.

When to Use

  • After completing a physician VOC interview
  • Processing raw transcript text into usable intelligence
  • Creating deliverable summary for stakeholders
  • Documenting KOL feedback for competitive intelligence

Pre-Processing Requirements

Tone & Formatting Standards (MANDATORY)

Per SOUL.md v3.1:

  • Write prose without bullets, numbered lists, or excessive bolding unless explicitly requested
  • Bullets (if used) should be 1-2 sentences minimum — not single words or fragments
  • Use the minimum formatting needed for clarity
  • Natural tone over structured formatting unless content is truly multifaceted

Device Name Normalization (MANDATORY)

Before processing any VOC transcript:

  1. Load memory/wp-product-names-reference.json
  2. Run wp_product_normalizer.py to scan for device names
  3. Apply all corrections to match official WP spelling
  4. Document any corrections in the "Notes" section

Critical spellings:

  • p48, p64 (lowercase p, no hyphen)
  • pNOVUS, pCONUS, pEGASUS, pRESET, pRELAX, pITA, pORTAL (lowercase p)
  • Avenir (capitalized, no prefix)
  • Esperance (capitalized, no prefix)
  • phenox (lowercase, company name)
  • HPC, ATUS, MW, LT (uppercase suffixes, space before)

See: memory/wp-product-names-reference.md for full list.

Content Standards

DO

  • Capture specific quotes where impactful
  • Include numerical data (percentages, volumes, counts)
  • Attribute specific device names and competitor mentions
  • Note pain points and unmet needs
  • Document collaboration opportunities
  • Include next steps or action items
  • Use professional, summary language (not raw transcript)

DO NOT

  • Copy-paste raw transcript
  • Include small talk, technical difficulties, or tangents
  • Leave ambiguous statements without clarification
  • Omit negative feedback or criticisms
  • Use informal language or slang from conversation

Quality Gates

Gate 1: Completeness

  • All major topics from conversation captured?
  • Specific devices/competitors named?
  • Numerical data included where mentioned?
  • Quotes captured for key insights?

Gate 2: Accuracy

  • Physician name and institution correct?
  • Attendees listed correctly?
  • No misattributed statements?
  • Technical details accurate?

Gate 3: Professionalism

  • Raw transcript converted to professional summary?
  • Informal language removed?
  • Tangents and small talk excluded?
  • Document formatted correctly?

Gate 4: Actionability

  • Next steps documented?
  • Collaboration opportunities noted?
  • Pain points clearly identified?
  • Competitive intelligence captured?
