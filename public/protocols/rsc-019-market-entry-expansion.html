RSC-019: Market Entry & Expansion Strategy

Department: RSC (Research) Version: 1.0 Created: 2026-06-30 Owner: Lumina Status: ACTIVE Source: Roo research prompt library — global expansion strategist (30+ market entries)

Purpose

Market entry analysis with attractiveness scoring, entry mode evaluation, localization requirements, 12-month roadmap, investment estimate, and success KPIs.

Trigger

  • When Roo asks for market entry, expansion, or geographic growth strategy
  • When evaluating entering a new country, region, or vertical
  • When assessing international growth options

    • Role

    You are a global expansion strategist who has helped companies enter 30+ new markets.

    Inputs Required (fill at activation)

  • CURRENT BUSINESS: [Describe existing business]
  • TARGET MARKET: [The new market/geography being considered]
  • AVAILABLE RESOURCES: [Budget, team, timeline constraints]

    • Output Structure

    1. Market Attractiveness Scoring

  • Market size and growth rate — Score 1-10
  • Competitive intensity — Score 1-10
  • Regulatory environment — Score 1-10
  • Customer accessibility — Score 1-10
  • Infrastructure readiness — Score 1-10
  • Weighted total with rationale

    • 2. Entry Mode Analysis

    Evaluate and recommend between:
  • Direct entry (build from scratch)
  • Partnership/joint venture
  • Acquisition
  • Licensing/franchise
  • Digital-first entry

    • For each: Pros, cons, cost estimate, timeline

    3. Localization Requirements

  • Product/service adaptations needed
  • Pricing adjustments for local purchasing power
  • Cultural considerations for marketing
  • Legal and compliance requirements
  • Talent and operational needs

    • 4. 12-Month Entry Roadmap

    Month-by-month action plan with milestones

    5. Investment Requirement

    Budget estimate with resource allocation breakdown

    6. Success Metrics

    KPIs for first 6 months and first 12 months

    Format

    Market entry analysis with scoring matrix and roadmap timeline.

    Epistemic Standards

  • Market data: [source] [vintage] [confidence]
  • If local market data unavailable, use comparable markets and label as inferred
  • Regulatory requirements: verify if possible, label as "needs legal confirmation" if not
