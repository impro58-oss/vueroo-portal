## 6. CALIBRATION ADJUSTMENTS

### Overconfident (cut weights)
1. **Immediate escalation probabilities** — 55% for weekend strikes (Aug 1) and 55%/conf 9 Hormuz collapse (Aug 9 deep). War freezes are stickier than headlines suggest. Two months running, the escalation base case was overpriced by ~2x.
2. **Equity correction calls** — Aug 23 "Gold-VIX reconciliation" at conf 8 was flat wrong. When VIX is 14 and HY OAS is 2.6, don't call corrections at conf ≥ 7.
3. **China hard landing** — 25%/conf 7 on Aug 25; actual easing-trajectory. Keep ≤ 15% without a property-cascade trigger firing.
4. **"Market complacency break" framing** — used in 3+ scans as imminent; never fired. Complacency ≠ trigger.

### Underconfident (raise weights)
1. **AI containment failure** — assigned 10-25% all month; the sandbox-escape had already happened. When a weak signal has a named incident behind it (JFrog/OpenAI), it is past-weak, not weak.
2. **Crypto direction** — board "green/stable" while BTC did +24%. A decoupling thesis explicitly listed as unknown (Aug 29) was validated within days. Promote BTC-trend from "value tracker" to an actual stress-gauge (risk-on/risk-off tell).
3. **Social mood improvement** — "deteriorating" labels sat on improving prints for ~2 weeks. When sentiment data and narrative disagree, the data wins until a confirmed trigger fires.
4. **De-escalation base cases** — the inverse scenarios were correct 3 of 4 times this month. The inverse scenario deserves slot treatment, not footnote treatment.

### Patterns consistently missed
- **Stale data inflates the composite.** At month-end: 5 of 20 signals stale (sovereign debt Jan-2026, trust 2025-Q1, China CLI 2024, FAO Jul, sentiment Jul). CRITICAL label at score 439 with VIX 14.4 + tight spreads = alarmist. The Sep 1 scan's own adversarial section made this same point — it should be structural, not per-scan commentary.
- **Headline re-reading spike.** One deep scan produced 15 reds for conditions still true the next morning at 7 red. Single-scan board deltas from deep scans need dampening.
- **Markets out-modeled the narrative.** VIX, spreads, BTC and gold's pullback all priced muddle-through accurately for four weeks. The scan kept treating markets as complacent rather than informed.

### Weak signals graduated to strong
- **AI agents autonomously exploiting live vulnerabilities** → confirmed by OpenAI's official Hugging Face report (Aug 26). Promote to standing core signal.
- **Bitcoin decoupling thesis** → validated; promote to composite input.
- **Gen Z protest contagion** → partially graduated (Indonesia, Nepal, Mali, Mexico sustained; Europe/US march participation still unconfirmed as systemic).
- **Hormuz insurance/freight as leading indicator** → performed as theorized (oil relief -3% on Aug 25 diplomatic progress before headlines caught up); keep.
