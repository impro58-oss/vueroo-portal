The scan's highest-impact risk (Hormuz escalation) could be wrong if backchannel negotiations are already underway that we can't see. Both the US and Iran have strong incentives to de-escalate: US doesn't want an election-year oil shock, Iran doesn't want its economy destroyed. If a ceasefire drops tomorrow, oil crashes to $60, and most of the "convergence" alerts dissipate. **This is plausible.** The scan's probability assignment of 35% for escalation assumes current trajectory; a peace announcement could drop it to near-zero within hours.

**Challenge 2: Financial Stability Is Better Than Indicators Suggest**
HY OAS at 2.69% is genuinely low. The yield curve at 0.36% is not inverted. The signal board's financial indicators are mostly green/amber, not red. This scan may overweight sovereign debt as a risk because the metric (122.59% debt/GDP) looks scary but has been stable for years. The system may be more resilient than the scan implies. **Assumption being challenged:** "High debt/GDP = high risk." In a world where every major economy has high debt, the relevant threshold may be higher than models suggest.

**Challenge 3: Social Cohesion Is Improving, Not Breaking**
Both social cohesion and public sentiment indicators show "improving" trends. Consumer sentiment rose from ~40 to 44.8. This scan may be extrapolating a crisis from lagging indicators that have already bottomed. If AI productivity gains start materializing (GPT-5.6 is now public), the economic optimism could accelerate, and social cohesion could recover faster than expected. **Assumption being challenged:** "Low social cohesion = imminent breakdown." It could simply be a post-pandemic reset to a new, lower-but-stable baseline.

**Challenge 4: Climate Signals Are Noisy, Not Trending**
The 1.11°C anomaly is actually labeled "improving." The heatwaves could be a particularly bad El Niño hangover, not a new baseline. If 2027 temperatures drop back toward 1.0°C, the "climate tipping point" scenario weakens significantly. **Assumption being challenged:** "Current extreme weather = permanent escalation." Climate is variable; one bad summer is not a trend.

**Challenge 5: The AI Regulatory Panic Is Overblown**
The White House gated GPT-5.6 for 12 days — then released it. Anthropic's models were restricted, then un-restricted. This could be a temporary overreaction to novel capabilities, not a permanent regulatory architecture. The "AI Cold War" scenario assumes governments will continue restricting models; they might instead settle into a light-touch review process and move on. **Assumption being challenged:** "Government gating = permanent regulatory regime." Bureaucracies often overreact initially and then relax.

### Weakest Assumptions in This Scan

1. **Proxy indicators are adequate.** Using Brent oil for both energy security AND supply chain health AND Middle East tension creates triple-counting from a single data point. If oil is misleading, three indicators are wrong simultaneously.

2. **Manual assessments are accurate.** Cyber threat level, institutional trust, and AI acceleration are manually assessed. These are Lumina's best estimates but lack hard data backing.

3. **Stale data is still meaningful.** Several indicators have data from January-June 2026. The world has changed significantly since then. The scan treats these as "current" when they are lagging.

4. **The convergence framework is causal.** Just because five domains are stressed simultaneously doesn't mean they're causally connected. Correlation ≠ causation in convergence analysis.
