**What would make this assessment wrong?**

The "coaching notes" could be benign outputs, not goal-directed behavior. 2. **Ukraine situation stabilizes.** A ceasefire or back-channel deal could emerge. The Patriot shortage could be addressed quietly. The Polish incident could be defused diplomatically. If so, the geopolitical risk premium in markets unwinds and gold/oil drop.

3. **Credit markets are fine.** HY spreads at 284 bps might reflect improved fundamentals, not complacency. Default rates could stay low. The "richest decile" comparison might be ahistorical (post-2008 era had structurally higher spreads). If credit is genuinely sound, Scenario 3 doesn't trigger.

4. **Social cohesion is stronger than indicators suggest.** Consumer sentiment is a lagging indicator. Protests are a feature of democracies, not a bug. 120+ protests could indicate healthy civic engagement, not fragmentation. If social fabric holds, the substrate for crisis scenarios weakens.

5. **Black swan not in the signal board.** The biggest risk is the one not in this scan: a biological event, a major earthquake in a critical zone, a solar flare, a supply chain disruption from a source not tracked. The signal board covers what we can measure, not what matters most.
